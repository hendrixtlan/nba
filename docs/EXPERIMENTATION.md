# Experimentation Strategy

Offline model accuracy does not prove commercial impact.

## Phase 1 — shadow mode
Run NBA decisions without changing business treatment. Validate latency, eligibility rules, action mix and operational safety.

## Phase 2 — randomized controlled experiment
Randomly assign eligible population units to control and treatment. Define primary metrics before launch. Prefer incremental margin or conversion uplift over raw conversion rate.

## Phase 3 — heterogeneous treatment effects
Estimate which customers are persuadable rather than merely likely to buy. Compare uplift/Qini-oriented evaluation with the baseline propensity strategy.

## Phase 4 — portfolio optimization
Optimize actions jointly under inventory, campaign budget, contact capacity and fairness/governance constraints.
